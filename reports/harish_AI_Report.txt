### 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

*   **Tutorial Hell:** Getting stuck in an endless loop of tutorials without applying knowledge to personal projects. Always build something after learning a concept.
*   **Ignoring Fundamentals:** Skipping data structures, algorithms, and core computer science concepts. These are the bedrock of good software engineering.
*   **Lack of Practice:** Not consistently coding and solving problems. Programming is a skill developed through consistent practice.
*   **Fear of Failure/Debugging:** Expect errors and bugs. They are opportunities to learn and improve problem-solving skills. Embrace debugging.
*   **Not Using Version Control (Git):** Failing to use Git from the beginning makes collaboration difficult and project management messy.
*   **Isolating Yourself:** Not engaging with the coding community, asking questions, or seeking feedback. Collaboration is key in software development.
*   **Neglecting Soft Skills:** Technical skills are crucial, but communication, teamwork, and problem-solving are equally important for career progression.
*   **Burning Out:** Overworking without breaks can lead to exhaustion. Maintain a healthy work-life balance.
*   **Chasing Every New Technology:** Focus on mastering core concepts and a few essential technologies rather than superficially learning many.

### 9. Daily Study Routine (Approximate for a high school student)

This routine should be flexible and adapt to school schedules.

*   **Morning (30 mins - 1 hour):**
    *   **Review/Plan (15 mins):** Briefly review notes from the previous day or plan the coding tasks for the current day.
    *   **Quick Coding Challenge (15-45 mins):** Solve one small problem on a platform like LeetCode (easy) or HackerRank.
*   **After School/Evening (1.5 - 2 hours):**
    *   **Theoretical Learning (45 mins):** Read a book, watch a tutorial, or take an online course on a new concept (e.g., a specific data structure, a new Python module, a CS fundamental).
    *   **Practical Coding/Project Work (1 hour):** Apply what was learned. Work on a personal project, implement an algorithm, or expand on a previous project.
    *   **Review & Plan (15 mins):** Briefly summarize what was learned, commit code to Git, and outline goals for the next study session.
*   **Weekend Focus:** Dedicate larger blocks of time for in-depth project work, attending workshops, or participating in online hackathons.
